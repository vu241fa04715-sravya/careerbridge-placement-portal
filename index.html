HOW IT WORKS

Our Placement Process

A structured recruitment process designed to prepare students for successful careers.

01
Student Registration

Students register with the placement cell and provide their academic details.

02
Eligibility Screening

Student eligibility is verified according to the company's recruitment criteria.

03
Aptitude Assessment

Candidates participate in aptitude and online assessments.

04
Technical Interview

Shortlisted candidates attend technical interviews with recruiters.

05
HR Interview

Candidates participate in the final HR and behavioral evaluation.

06
Final Selection

Selected students receive offers from the recruiting organization.
