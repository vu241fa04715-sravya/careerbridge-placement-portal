WHO WE ARE

Building Bridges Between Campus & Industry

The Placement Cell at CareerBridge acts as a bridge between students and the corporate world.

Our team works closely with leading organizations to provide students with meaningful career opportunities while preparing them for the expectations of the modern workplace.

Through structured training, industry interactions, career guidance, aptitude preparation and interview support, we help students develop the confidence and skills required to succeed in competitive recruitment processes.

RECRUITMENT JOURNEY

How Our Placement Process Works

01
Registration

Students register with the placement cell and provide required information.

02
Eligibility

Student profiles are checked against company eligibility requirements.

03
Assessment

Candidates participate in aptitude, coding or online assessments.

04
Technical Interview

Shortlisted candidates participate in technical interviews.

05
HR Interview

Final candidates complete the HR and behavioral evaluation.

06
Selection

Successful candidates receive their final placement offers.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Placement FAQs

The placement process generally includes student registration, eligibility screening, aptitude or technical assessment, technical interviews, HR interviews and final selection.

Eligibility depends on the recruitment requirements specified by each company. Academic performance, department, graduation year and other criteria may be considered.

Students can participate in aptitude training, coding preparation, technical sessions, communication training, resume building, mock interviews and group discussions.

Companies can contact the Placement Cell to discuss recruitment requirements, eligibility criteria, recruitment schedules and campus hiring opportunities.

The Placement Cell provides career guidance, training programs, recruitment information, interview preparation and coordination with recruiting organizations.
